1. Information we process

Only information that you enter or import in this app is recorded in the ledger, namely:

  • Profile: nickname and gender. Gender may be left undisclosed. A nickname is not required in order to use the app.
  • Preferences: interface language and currency.
  • Ledger records: account name and opening balance; and, for each transaction, its name, amount, direction, category, date, account, whether it has been checked, and whether it is included in the household view.
  • Holdings: code, name, quantity, cost, price and price date. These figures are entered by you. This app does not query a market data service.
  • Budgets: the budget for the current month and category budgets.
  • Categories you add, and the foregoing categories of information contained in a JSON backup that you import.

On first launch, this app may load a sample ledger so that you can see how the functions work. After you use Clear or Clear data, that sample ledger is not loaded again.

We do not collect telephone numbers, email addresses, identity document numbers, contacts, precise location, photographs, advertising identifiers or device identifiers. This app does not offer registration, sign-in, verification by text message or sign-in through a third-party account, and it does not include advertising or usage-statistics software.

2. Purposes of use

The information described above is used solely to provide the corresponding functions on that device, including recording income and expenditure, displaying balances and net worth, filtering by time and category, calculating the remaining budget, and displaying the value of holdings.

The household designation is a viewing scope within the ledger on that device. It does not transmit records to any other person or device.

We do not use such information for advertising, profiling, credit assessment or automated decision-making, and we do not sell your ledger.

3. Storage location and retention

The ledger is stored in the app’s private directory, in a local database. Uninstalling the app, or clearing data under Data in Me, deletes that ledger. After the data are cleared, the sample ledger is not written again.

If a ledger written by an earlier version remains in system preferences on the device, the app will, on first launch, migrate it into the database and delete the earlier record. That migration takes place only on the device.

The retention period is determined by you. Until you clear the data or uninstall the app, the ledger remains on the device so that you may continue it when you next open the app.

4. Sharing and third parties

We do not operate a server that receives the ledger, and we therefore do not provide it to advertisers, data brokers or a bank interface. Bookkeeping, balances and budgets are calculated on the device.

In the browser version, the ledger is written to that site’s local storage and is likewise not uploaded. Clearing that site’s data deletes the ledger in the browser version.

5. Your management of the information

  • Access and correction: you may open the relevant account, transaction, holding, nickname or budget and amend it.
  • Removal of the sample ledger: Clear on Overview, Transactions or Accounts removes the preset sample. Records that you have entered thereafter are retained.
  • Deletion of the ledger: Clear data under data management deletes accounts, transactions, holdings and budgets. Category names, nickname, gender, language and currency are retained. The sample ledger is not loaded again.
  • Uninstallation: uninstalling the app deletes the ledger stored in the app’s private directory.

This app does not operate an account server. We cannot retrieve or delete a ledger for you from a remote service. The information is managed through the on-device operations described above.

6. Protection of on-device storage

Bookkeeping and the calculation of balances are performed on the device. The ledger is not transmitted to us. We do not retain a further copy, and we therefore cannot consult, restore or delete it on your behalf.

The degree of protection depends on the device, including whether a screen lock is set, who is able to unlock it, and whether you enable the system backup. If the device is backed up, the backup may include the ledger stored in this app. That backup is handled by the operating system and the backup service you select. We do not operate that backup and cannot inspect its contents.

If the device is lost and no backup remains under your control, we cannot restore the ledger.

7. Children

This app is intended for adults who keep their own accounts. It is not directed at children under the age of 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Please do not record a child’s identity document number or other sensitive identifying information in the ledger.

A parent or guardian may amend or clear the ledger on the device, or uninstall the app. We do not retain a server copy that could be deleted remotely.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy is revised, the revised text will be published on the Privacy page in this app, and the date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly. That date is the publication date and the effective date of that version. From that date, the revised text supersedes the previous version.

This app does not maintain user accounts or a list of user email addresses, and therefore cannot send a separate notice of a policy change to each user. Publication on this page constitutes notice of the change. Your continued use of this app after the date shown at the top constitutes your having read the version bearing that date.
